🌍 Minecraft — Quick Guide & Crafting Helper

Basics, controls, mobs, and a tiny interactive crafting helper — single-file HTML

What is Minecraft?

Minecraft is a sandbox game about placing and breaking blocks. Players explore, gather resources, craft tools, build structures, and survive threats. It has creative and survival modes, multiplayer servers, and a massive modding community.

Game Modes

Survival: gather food, manage health and hunger, craft tools, and fight mobs. Creative: infinite resources and flying for building. Adventure: play custom maps (restrictions on block breaking).

Common Controls (PC)

W A S D move — Space jump — Shift sneak — E inventory — 1–9 hotbar. Mouse left-click to break/place; right-click to use/place items.

Tip: Sprint by double-tapping W or holding Ctrl (configurable).

Common Mobs

Creeper
Explodes near player — avoid or use a bow.
Zomb ie
Hostile at night, burns in daylight.
Skeleton
Ranged attacker with bow.
Enderman
Tall neutral mob — don't stare.
Villager
Trades items; breed them with food.
Spider
Climbs walls; hostile at night.

Beginner Tips

  • First night: gather wood, craft a crafting table and wooden tools, build a simple shelter.
  • Always carry a stack of torches when mining to stop mobs spawning.
  • Make a bed to skip night and reset your spawn point (requires wool + wood).
  • Use a hoe + seeds to start a small farm for food security.
